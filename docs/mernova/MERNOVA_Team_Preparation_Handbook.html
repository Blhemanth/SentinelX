1. 5-Day Intensive Team Preparation Sprint

Day-by-Day Training Syllabus 5-Day Schedule

DAY 1: Architecture Mastery & Fact Alignment
• Goal: Every team member achieves 100% fluency in SentinelX technical numbers and boundaries.
• Activities: Group read-through of SentinelX_Project_Master_Guide.pdf. Memorize key telemetry numbers: 238 passing tests, 10-minute test metrics (4,399 frames, 229 inferences, 0.38 FPS, 7.33 FPS camera, 97.53% fluidity, 416 MB RSS).
• Verification Gate: Rapid-fire oral quiz on the 5-tier evidence classification.

DAY 2: Live Demo Operations & Timing Synchronization
• Goal: Demo operator achieves flawless muscle memory for the 2:20 minute live demo.
• Activities: Practice cold startup sequence 5 times. Rehearse window arrangement, camera motion drill, and incident triage flow. Coordinate screen transitions with Presenter 3's spoken lines.
• Verification Gate: Complete 3 consecutive demo runs within exactly 2:15–2:25 without a single verbal pause or misclick.

DAY 3: Judges Q&A Defense Drills (Simulated Grilling)
• Goal: Eliminate hesitation and defensive posturing when answering difficult technical questions.
• Activities: Presenters take turns sitting in the "hot seat" while peer teammates roleplay aggressive, skeptical judges using questions from MERNOVA_Judges_QA_Handbook.pdf.
• Verification Gate: Answer 25 random questions from Categories 1–15, strictly stating the correct Tier badge and avoiding over-promising.

DAY 4: Full Dress Rehearsals with Injected Faults
• Goal: Master contingency protocols under high-stress conditions.
• Activities: Conduct 4 complete 7-minute timed dress rehearsals in full presentation attire. Teammate deliberately injects faults during the demo (unplugging camera, dropping network, toggling slow CPU clock). Presenter 3 executes rehearsed contingency spoken pivots seamlessly.
• Verification Gate: Full 7-minute presentation finishes between 6:45 and 6:58 despite an injected fault.

DAY 5: Final Polish, Physical Pack & Stage Readiness
• Goal: Complete operational readiness and mental calm.
• Activities: Equipment packing checklist verification. Clean laptop reboot. Test HDMI projector mirroring and audio levels. Final team pep talk and strategy lock.
• Verification Gate: All 6 PDF guides loaded onto offline tablets/phones; demo laptop sealed in flight bag.

5. Mock-Jury Interrogation Simulation Scenarios

Tough Judge Drill: Judges in competitive hackathons and engineering juries probe for weak spots. Prepare your team for these five challenging jury confrontation scenarios:

Scenario 1: The Skeptical AI Researcher
Judge: "At 0.38 FPS, your model processes one frame sequence every 2.6 seconds. Isn't that far too slow to catch a fast-moving physical assault?"
Winning Team Response (Presenter 2): "That is a perceptive observation. In surveillance anomaly classification, violent altercations and stampedes span several seconds. However, our 0.38 FPS throughput is a consequence of running 34M parameters on CPU AVX2. Our sliding temporal buffer samples 16 frames continuously, meaning we detect macro-motion patterns accurately. In Phase 4.4, compiling DirectML GPU execution providers will accelerate throughput to > 15 FPS."

Scenario 2: The Cynical Security Auditor
Judge: "If a hacker gains physical access to a surveillance PC, can they forge fake emergency dispatches into your backend?"
Winning Team Response (Presenter 2): "Absolutely not. In our Phase 4.3.5.1 security trust boundary audit, we proved that desktop client binaries contain zero HMAC secrets. Any payload submitted by a client is strictly stripped of emergency flags and demoted by Express middleware to an unverified 'manual_entry' state, requiring human operator confirmation before dispatch."

Scenario 3: The Deployment Pragmatist
Judge: "Why should a mall or airport invest in SentinelX instead of established giants like Milestone or Genetec?"
Winning Team Response (Presenter 1): "Traditional enterprise VMS platforms charge expensive proprietary licensing fees and lack native edge multi-modal threat classification. SentinelX is edge-native, open-architecture, costs a fraction of proprietary systems, and eliminates the $90,000/year cloud bandwidth overhead of cloud-first surveillance."
